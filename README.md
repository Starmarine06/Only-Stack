# Only-Stack
[![Version](https://img.shields.io/static/v1?label=Version&message=1.1.0v&color=blue)]()

Ever wish mining was less about constantly filling your inventory and more about massive, efficient hauls? Are you tired of throwing one item at a time, wishing you could just launch a full stack with a single toss? 
This datapack is for you! It supercharges your item drops and throws, turning every action into a stack-sized event. When you break a block, instead of a single item, a full stack (x64) is dropped. Whether you're mining stone, chopping wood, or farming crops, you’ll get a massive boost to your resource collection. And it doesn't stop there. When you throw an item, it instantly becomes a full stack. Need to share some resources with a friend? One throw is all it takes to give them a stack of whatever you’re holding. Streamline your resource gathering and say goodbye to tedious, piecemeal collection. It's time to build bigger, farm faster, and trade smarter.

## Features

- Blocks always drop a full stack (x64) when broken.
- Thrown items instantly become a full stack (x64).
- ## Installation

  And press download datapack.(No resource pack required)
